The problem

Every professional has done this at least once.

You needed to send a sensitive document to a client fast. So you put it on a USB drive — or emailed it — or used a personal Dropbox link. Not because you didn't care about security. Because the secure option was too complicated, too slow, or would have required your client to install something they didn't want to.

That five-minute shortcut is where most confidential data gets exposed.

How it works

Simple for you. One click for them.

The entire process — from protecting your files to your client opening them — takes less time than writing an email.

--- SENDER SIDE ---

*

Before you start:

After purchasing FileLocker, you receive a license key by email. Enter it once to activate the software. Takes 30 seconds. You never need to do it again.

*

Optional — Set up your firm's branding:

Add your firm's name, logo, and brand colors to FileLocker. When your client opens their files, they see your firm's branding — not ours. It looks like your own professional secure delivery system.

01

Choose where to save:

Select any USB drive plugged into your computer. FileLocker shows you all available drives with their available storage. Pick the right one and continue.

02

Select your documents:

Browse your computer and choose the file or folder you need to send. Any format, any size — up to 10 gigabytes and beyond. If you select a folder, FileLocker automatically bundles everything inside it.

Two optional settings most professionals love:
  • Auto-delete the original file from your computer after locking — so no unprotected copy remains
  • Hide the real file name — so even the document name stays private (e.g., your client's file appears as "Secure_Data" instead of "Divorce_Settlement.pdf")
03

Create a password:

Type a strong password — or let FileLocker generate one for you automatically. Add an optional hint to help your client remember without giving away the password itself. The password is never saved anywhere on your computer or any server.

04

Lock and deliver:

Click Lock Files. FileLocker protects your documents and saves everything to the USB drive in seconds. A green confirmation screen confirms it worked. Hand the drive to your client.

--- RECIPIENT SIDE ---

01

Plug in and open:

Your client inserts the USB drive and double-clicks one file. It opens instantly in their web browser.

02

No software needed. At all:

Nothing downloads. Nothing installs. No account required. If they can open a website, they can open their documents.

Note: Your client will see your firm's name and logo on this screen — not FileLocker's. It looks like your own professional system.

03

Enter the password:

Your client types the password you shared with them separately (by phone or text — never on the USB itself). The optional hint you added appears here if they need a reminder.

04

Documents saved:

Their browser unlocks the files entirely on their computer — no data is sent to any server. A save dialog appears and their documents are saved directly to their device. Done.

!

Important note about sharing the password:

Share the password separately from the USB drive. Call your client, text them, or tell them in person. Never write the password on the drive or include it in the same package. This separation is what makes the delivery genuinely secure.

FAQ

Questions answered honestly.

Does my client need to install any software?+

No — and that is the whole point. Your client opens one file in their web browser and types a password. Nothing downloads. Nothing installs. No account required. If they can visit a website, they can open their documents.

What happens if the USB drive is lost or stolen?+

Nothing — and this is exactly what FileLocker is for. The files on the drive are protected with military-grade encryption. Without the correct password, whoever finds the drive cannot read a single byte of the contents. Mathematically guaranteed.

What if I forget the password?+

Because FileLocker works completely offline with no servers involved, there is no password recovery option. This is a security feature, not a limitation — a recovery system would mean someone else could access your files. We strongly recommend using the built-in password generator and saving the result in your password manager before clicking Lock. The optional hint feature also helps your client remember without giving away the password itself.

Does my client need to know anything about technology?+

If they can open a website and type a word, they can open their files. The unlock screen is designed for non-technical clients. It has one field, one button, and your firm's branding on the screen.

Does FileLocker work on Mac?+

The desktop app for locking files runs on Windows 10 and 11. Your clients can unlock files on Windows, Mac, or Linux — any computer with a modern browser. macOS sender support is on our roadmap.

How do I share the password with my client?+

Share it separately from the USB drive — call them, text them, or tell them in person. Never write the password on the drive or include it in the same package. Keeping the key separate from the lock is what makes this genuinely secure.

Can I send entire folders — not just single files?+

Yes. Select any folder and FileLocker automatically bundles everything inside it before locking. Your client receives the complete folder structure when they unlock.

Is my data stored anywhere?+

No. FileLocker operates entirely offline. No file, no password, and no document name ever touches any server. There is no cloud component and no internet connection required at any point — for you or your client.
